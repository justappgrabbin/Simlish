### Huge files — what her originals do (checked first, then tested)
* **Cap is inherited.** `ExecutionAddressResolver.descend` keeps `sixBitStates.slice(0, 4096)` (`zips/05-Synthia-Universal-Execution-Spine-v0.4.0…/src/execution-address-resolver.mjs` line 87; identical to `integrated/spine/execution-address-resolver.mjs`, 4 copies in her zips). `sixBitStateCount`, `ones/zeros/parity/byteXor/byteSum/primitiveHash` are computed over ALL bytes; only the state *array* is cut. Real result, 20 000 bytes: needs 26 667 states, `sixBitStateCount` 26 667, array length 4096.
* **Split across dimensions exists** in her v0.5.1 pipeline: `fieldIndexForPrimitive = (state+index) % 5` → five fields (`zips/_drive_x/Synthia-v0.5.1-EXECUTION-PIPELINE-PATCH/packages/execution-spine/src/execution-pipeline.mjs` lines 61-65, `allocateFiveFields`). Measured on 3073 bytes: Movement 818 · Evolution 818 · Being 821 · Design 811 · Space 828 (4096 total; 4098 needed).
* **Her original pipeline cannot run files over ~2 KB: a defect in her `dag-runtime.mjs`** (one copy in 10 places, sha `7ecf09727e`): `checkpoint()` does `structuredClone` of all statuses/results after **every** node → O(n²). Measured with her pipeline (stub core, 1 GB heap): 300 B 0.5 s · 600 B 1.8 s · 1200 B **9.4 s** · 3000 B, 3073 B, 20 000 B **FATAL ERROR: JavaScript heap out of memory**. (Earlier, `IntegratedSwarm.execute` on 20 000 bytes also ran out of a 4 GB heap after 70 s.)
* **Repair (only this):** `integrated/spine/dag-runtime.mjs` now takes ≤ ~64 spaced checkpoints for graphs over 64 nodes (identical behaviour for ≤ 64 nodes). Her original is kept byte-for-byte as `spine/dag-runtime.original.mjs`; `MANIFEST.json` records both. After: 1200 B 0.98 s · 3000 B 4.7 s · 3073 B 5.0 s · 20 000 B 4.7 s (all complete; `large-file.test.mjs`). `IntegratedSwarm.execute` 20 000 B: 4.7 s, status investigating.
* **Second defect fixed (mine):** `fold.truncatedPipelineInput` said `false` for the 20 000-byte file although 22 563 states were dropped; `fold.sixBitStates {needed, scheduled, cappedByDescend}` now reports it.
* **Not done, and not invented:** nothing in her files executes states beyond 4096. Her ingestor reduces all bits (20 000 B → `primitiveCount` 160 000, five dimension scores, exact reconstruction because it stores the original bytes) but does not execute them. Windowing the rest through the pipeline would be a new mechanism → reported as missing.
