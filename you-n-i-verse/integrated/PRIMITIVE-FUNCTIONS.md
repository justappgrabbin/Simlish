## Ten most important gaps

1. Behavior of the chain lines / natures / keynotes / crystals / centers of all five dimensions (what "Energy is Creation" DOES): only the letter-level and hand-off functions exist (prototype).
2. Space: no emergence rule or readout function (her spec: read-only, emergent).
3. Being wheel (Eight Palaces / Taiji pivot) and Design wheel (AST-style structural parsing): no sequence table in any file; one candidate in her Drive doc 1MetULPQ… for Being.
4. Which Mawangdui reading is hers (A-E and Rutt side by side; Rutt's Table 10 is the only one tied to the silk manuscript in a source she holds).
5. Letter/base -> bigram rule (which bigram is the pack of 3; A/C/G/T -> 2 bits).
6. Colors, tones, bases: degree spans exist (her OntologicalAddress.js) but the behavior of a color mode or tone is only text (Colors book, doc3 §J,K).
7. Arc-second field: her theory that it differs by dimension is open.
8. Shapes: Xiang, glyph, Shaper, Shader are text only.
9. Ten Wings: ten names found (Rutt), only Shuogua is tied to a function.
10. Memory completion and DNA insertion: no function; DNA substitution has the new packSubstitute (derived, confirm); completion only has LexicalFillEngine as the nearest rule-based filler.
