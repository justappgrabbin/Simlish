# Assignment and integration contract

Current user instructions govern this contract. Historical assistant prose and donor implementations are evidence to inspect, not authority to override those instructions.

## Intended system

Simlish is the host. Its existing pages, characters, tools and interactions stay available. The swarm is the embodied, causal execution substrate: independently executable automata compose into working objects, actors, tools, language, interfaces and world conditions. Rendering shows the consequences of their state and relationships. A decorative particle layer does not satisfy the assignment.

Each primitive has persistent identity, intrinsic qualities, strengths, weaknesses, permitted movement, permitted operations and transitions. Higher compositions retain constituent references, addresses, relationships and full lineage. A composition may become a primitive at another scale without erasing the lower history.

Movement, Evolution, Being and Design contribute to one underlying addressed system. Space emerges through their interplay. It is not an extra independent model or an unrelated graph. The governing primitive establishes a composition's qualitative condition. Dimensional interpretation is contextual.

The user requests world flavors. The request must resolve into permissible changes in embodied conditions, composition, behavior and interactions. These changes alter subsequent game outcomes; they cannot merely recolor the screen or silently assign arbitrary capabilities.

Physical dynamics use explicit, dimensionally consistent rules with stated units and constraints. Morphological syntax governs legal ordered and unordered composition, recursive scale, context and transformations. Physical validity and grammatical validity must both hold before a change lands.

GA supplies the embodied composition/evolution side; PSO supplies the task/action search side. They operate in distinct phases, sharing retained state, not concurrently. A phase switch alone is not an implementation of either algorithm. Binary/polarity participates in primitive structure; the exact source-defined relationship to sound must be recovered, not guessed. Sound, shape and color resolve from the same qualified state through the mesh.

Alphabet, glyphs and definition/reveal filters remain in scope. They are ways to inspect and express the substrate, not the sole purpose of the swarm. B A N T U and the user's scale-dependent primitive rules must not be replaced by guessed letter mappings.

## Agency and cultivation

The units must actually execute work and exchange qualified packets. A unit's capability is constrained by its primitive composition and the person's design. Typed contracts prevent incompatible mesh connections. Roles must be resolved from qualities, position, context and relations, rather than granted by an arbitrary label.

Cultivation connects observations, tasks, consequences, memory, knowledge gaps and verified outcomes to future behavior. Human-defined purpose and success govern the process. The system complements the person rather than coercing them into maximizing an agent score.

Persistent science mode retains observations, hypotheses, alternatives, predicted outcomes, actual outcomes and research provenance across sessions. Rejected and inconclusive results remain part of the record. Higher-order local models are retained in the larger architecture; the initial Pure Synthia execution baseline remains browser-native JavaScript without mandatory backend, external LLM or GPU.

External opportunities and optional MCP contact mount onto the same organism through the supplied relationship/consent membrane. They do not create a second organism or become required for local operation.

## State and error boundaries

Birth data initializes a position within the existing system; it does not invent the ontology. No placeholder chart or hash-based address may be represented as a verified design calculation.

DMSA input must explicitly reject degree outside 0–29, minute or second outside 0–59, and fractional arc outside 0–99. Errors identify field, value, range, full input and requesting operation. Wheel coordinates and local DMSA are distinct representations requiring a documented conversion.

Every landed transition retains its causal parent, before/after state, parameters, address, dimension, context, responsible automata and provenance. Fallbacks remain observable. History must not be silently truncated. New evidence branches from earlier landed facts.

## Completion evidence

A complete vertical path is:

user request → addressed reduction → primitive/capability constraints → recursive composition → phase-specific automata work → mesh interaction → authoritative game-state transition → unified sensory/visual embodiment → consequence → human outcome/science observation → persistent cultivation.

The same particle identities must survive a flavor morph. A controlled interaction must produce different lawful outcomes under different requested conditions. Reload must restore the landed lineage. A prohibited action must fail observably. Rendered state, audio state and host-game state must agree. These checks distinguish completed integration from independent donor tests or a visual prototype.

The current `world.mjs` is a disclosed classical-physics demonstration. It does not yet satisfy the complete path above and must not be presented as the full swarm.
